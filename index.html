01 / ABOUT

Biology is complex.
Data helps us see it.

I am Md Sajid Nasim, a Project Scientist and PhD Scholar at IIT Ropar, working at the interface of multi-omics, genomics, transcriptomics, metagenomics, metabolomics, computational systems biology, machine learning and structural bioinformatics.

My background spans Bioinformatics and Zoology, with practical experience in high-throughput sequencing, multi-omics analysis, molecular docking and machine-learning approaches for biological data. My research interests include metagenomics, metabolomics, genomics, transcriptomics, bulk RNA-seq, single-cell RNA-seq, spatial transcriptomics, miRNA analysis and computational systems biology, with emphasis on functional, pathway and network-level interpretation.
